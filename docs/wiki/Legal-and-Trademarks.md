# Legal and trademarks

*This is how the project approaches legal questions. It isn't legal advice.*

## Reverse engineering

Headset Stats talks to the headset adapter by observing the USB messages it sends to a PC. This
was done on hardware we own, **for interoperability**: to make the headset's battery visible on
PCs. Interoperability is specifically allowed by the EU Software Directive (Art. 6) and the US DMCA
(§1201(f)). No copy protection is circumvented, and the headset's firmware is never modified.

The project never includes Sony software, firmware, SDK code, logos, or product photos.
