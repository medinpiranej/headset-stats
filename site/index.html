Free beta · Windows 10 & 11

Your headset's battery, right in the Windows tray.

Windows treats your headset's USB adapter as a plain sound card and never shows its battery. Headset Stats reads the adapter's status messages and shows battery, charging, mic mute and button presses next to the clock.

Works with the PlayStation® PULSE 3D™ wireless headset. Other Sony headsets can be tried experimentally.

Everything at a glance

The tray icon shows the battery level and changes colour as it drains, with a bolt while charging, a badge when your mic is muted, and grey when the headset is off. Vector-drawn and sharp at every size and display scaling.

🧪 Your headset isn't listed?

With no supported adapter plugged in, Headset Stats listens (read-only) to other Sony devices and tries the PULSE 3D message format, which similar adapters may share. Press Report a device on GitHub… in the app to send us the logs. That's usually all we need to add it.

What to expect

The headset reports its battery in 10 % steps, reads high for a while after charging (the app marks it as "settling"), and only sends status when something changes. The app explains all of this in its About tab.

How it works

Next to its audio interface, the adapter has a small data channel (USB HID). Whenever something changes, it sends an 8-byte status message. Headset Stats listens and decodes it, and never sends anything to your headset.

The byte layout was worked out by recording the messages while pressing every button, switching the headset on and off and plugging the cable in and out. The notes and recordings are public.

B0 05 28 32 EF 11 11 64
   │  │  │  │  │     └ volume 100 %
   │  │  │  │  └ event: volume up
   │  │  │  └ flags: on, mic live
   │  │  └ battery 50 %
   │  └ game/chat balance: centred
   └ volume level

Join the beta

Headset Stats is on its way to the Microsoft Store as a free beta for Windows 10 (1809+) and Windows 11.

  1. Install Headset Stats from the Microsoft Store (live once Microsoft approves the beta), or run winget install 9P8NLJ432R5R. Updates arrive automatically.
  2. Use it with your headset for a few days.
  3. Tell us how it goes: open an issue, and paste the output of Copy device logs from the app.

Can't wait? Download the latest build from GitHub Actions (Artifacts of the newest run), or build it yourself.
